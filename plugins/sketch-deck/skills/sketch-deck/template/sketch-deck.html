Sketch the deck, Claude draws it

  1. Draw each slide's content with the pen.
  2. Frame each slide (F, then drag). Frames are numbered in the order you draw them.
  3. Use the orange note pen, or Text (T) to type, for instructions, like "bar chart here" or "use our logo". Notes don't show when presenting.
  4. Paste (Ctrl+V) or drop images as examples of the look you want. They're references for Claude and don't show when presenting.
  5. Select (V) to move, resize, delete or duplicate. Moving a frame takes its content along.
  6. Slides (S) to insert, delete and reorder slides; the grid re-flows and renumbers. Steps (L) to order what appears when on a slide.
  7. When you're done, tell Claude in the chat to turn the sketch into visuals.
  8. Download saves the slides as a standalone presentation (.html), or as layers for Inkscape (.svg).
